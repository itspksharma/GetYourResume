HELP & INFORMATION

Questions, answered.

A few practical things to know before you apply.

How does GetYourResume build a resume?

Enter your contact information, skills, education, work experience, and projects through guided questions. Add a target job if useful, review AI suggestions or edit manually, then choose a template and download your PDF.

Can I continue without AI?

Yes. Resume assembly, manual editing, job matching, version management, and PDF download work without an AI request. Hosted AI depends on Cloudflare availability and its shared free allocation.

Where is my resume information stored?

Your workspace stays in this browser’s localStorage. It is not encrypted and can be lost if browser data is cleared. Use JSON export for backups. Only the facts you review and relevant job text are sent to Cloudflare when you request AI assistance.

What do the PDF templates include?

Three single-column layouts use black text, standard headings, and selectable text. ATS Classic uses Times-compatible Liberation Serif. Modern Professional and Developer Portfolio use Arial-compatible Liberation Sans. Downloads do not include site branding, browser headers, print dates, or page numbers. User-entered dates and links are preserved.

Does a compatibility score predict hiring or ATS acceptance?

No. It is an explained comparison of recognized job skill requirements with text evidence in your profile. It is not an official ATS score, a proficiency assessment, or a hiring prediction. Follow the employer’s format and application instructions.
